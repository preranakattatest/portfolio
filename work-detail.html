THE BRIEF & OBJECTIVE

The challenge was to design and deliver a fully functional CO₂ transmitter enclosure within an accelerated 2–3 day turnaround time, working around existing internal electronic boards, display screens, and sensor components.

This required rapid translation of component dimensional requirements into a manufacturable, protective, and well-ventilated enclosure shell that could be immediately fabricated via 3D printing.

LEARNING THROUGH ITERATION

  • VENTILATION OPTIMISATION

    Transitioned from simple rectangular slots to hexagonal openings. This significantly improved printability by avoiding horizontal bridging while refining the overall industrial aesthetic.

  • PRINTABILITY & GEOMETRY

    Redesigned interior overhangs and adjusted print orientation to completely eliminate the need for difficult internal support geometry.

  • FIT & ASSEMBLY

    Refined standoff spacer heights and screen seating dimensions; removed an unnecessary sticker groove to simplify printing tolerances and speed up assembly.
