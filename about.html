BIOGRAPHY & CAREER

MEET PETER

A multidisciplinary designer obsessed with raw typography, functional brutalism, and user experience that converts.

I believe the modern web has become sterile. The ubiquity of identical SaaS templates and generic design systems has created a web without character. My work injects tactile energy, bold analog grit, and deliberate contrast back into digital experiences.

Over the past eight years, I've served as lead product designer, creative director, and frontend collaborator for clients ranging from seed-stage founders to global lifestyle brands.

When I'm not pushing pixels in Figma or tinkering with code, you'll find me digging through dusty vinyl record crates, experimenting with analog 35mm photography, and restoring vintage synthesizers.
