THE BRIEF & OBJECTIVE

Design and deliver a functional CO₂ transmitter enclosure within an accelerated 2–3 day turnaround, working around fixed existing electronic boards and wiring. The goal was translating component requirements into a rugged, functional enclosure shell that could be immediately fabricated and deployed.

LEARNING THROUGH ITERATION

01. VENTILATION OPTIMISATION

Rectangular slots → Hexagonal openings: Rectangular cutouts created sagging horizontal bridge layers during 3D printing. Switching to hexagonal geometry self-supported the overhangs, improved printability, and refined the design aesthetics.

02. PRINTABILITY & ORIENTATION

Redesigned interior features and adjusted print orientation to overcome unsupported geometry, completely eliminating the need for difficult-to-remove internal support structures.

03. FIT & ASSEMBLY

Refined spacer heights and screen dimensions; removed the sticker groove to simplify printing tolerances and speed up assembly time.

THE OUTCOME
8 UNITS SHIPPED

A successful first project that taught me to design around real-world constraints, learn through rapid prototyping, and iterate quickly. The design continues to evolve with new customer requirements.
